# Week 08 - Recursive duration total

## Aim

Recursively total the three fixed duration values.

## Procedure

1. Store 184, 201, and 145 seconds in an array.
2. Use the end-of-array base case.
3. Add duration values recursively.
4. Compile, run, and compare output.

## Scope boundary

Durations are invented local fixtures and have no media connection.
