# Week 03 - Practice-play calculation

## Aim

Calculate the fixed practice-play total and use a decision rule.

## Procedure

1. Add the fixture play counts `3`, `1`, and `2`.
2. Calculate a two-decimal average with `Locale.ROOT`.
3. Apply the fixed STEADY badge rule at six plays.
4. Compile, run, and compare output.

## Scope boundary

Play counts are fictional learning markers, not personal listening data.
